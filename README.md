## 🌱 Inspiration

Mental well-being is an important part of everyday life, but people often don't take the time to check in with themselves or recognize when stress is beginning to affect their daily routine.

We wanted to build something simple, accessible, and non-judgmental that encourages people to regularly reflect on their well-being.

That's how **MindMate** was born.

MindMate is designed to make mental wellness check-ins feel as natural and accessible as checking the weather or tracking your daily activity.

---

## 🎯 What is MindMate?

MindMate is an AI-powered wellness companion that allows users to perform quick mental well-being check-ins.

The system analyzes user responses and provides:

- 🧠 Personalized well-being insights
- 📊 Stress and mood pattern analysis
- 💡 Personalized suggestions
- 🌱 Healthy habit recommendations
- 📅 Regular self-reflection
- 🤖 AI-powered conversational support

MindMate is **not a medical diagnosis or replacement for professional healthcare**. Its purpose is to encourage self-awareness and proactive well-being.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📝 Daily Check-ins

Users can quickly record how they are feeling and reflect on different aspects of their day.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Insights

AI analyzes check-in responses to identify potential patterns in mood, stress, and daily habits.

### 📊 Well-being Dashboard

Users can visualize their progress and observe changes in their well-being over time.

### 💡 Personalized Recommendations

Based on the user's responses, MindMate suggests practical activities and habits that may help improve their overall well-being.

### 💬 AI Wellness Companion

Users can interact with MindMate through a conversational interface to reflect on their thoughts and receive supportive guidance.

### 🔒 Privacy-Conscious Design

MindMate is designed with sensitive user information in mind. The system minimizes unnecessary data collection and avoids presenting AI-generated information as medical advice.
